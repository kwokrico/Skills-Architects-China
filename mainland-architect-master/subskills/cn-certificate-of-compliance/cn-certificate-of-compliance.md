# Mainland Completion Compliance and Filing
Execution playbook for project closeout under Mainland acceptance and filing workflows, with focus on multi-authority coordination and evidence integrity.

---

## 1. Core Position
- Treat completion filing as a multi-party closeout system, not a single submission event.
- Build backward from handover, operation, and commercial milestones.
- Lock handover evidence quality early; incomplete records are a common source of delay.
- Coordinate legal, surveying, engineering, and authority interfaces under one document control workflow.

---

## 2. Mainland Closeout Targets
- `竣工联合验收`: multi-authority acceptance coordination.
- `竣工验收备案`: formal filing completion for project closure.
- Land-condition closure (where applicable) for downstream commercial and asset-operation steps.
- Contractual completion and defects-handling activation.

---

## 3. Typical Trigger Conditions for Closeout
Common obligations to close before final filing/handover:
1. Building completion and compliance with approved plans.
2. Planning-condition and land-condition obligations closure.
3. Fire and special-system acceptance evidence completeness.
4. Municipal/public-space obligations and interface handover where required.
5. Required certificates, as-built records, and maintenance period obligations.

Control principle:
- Build a condition-by-condition closeout matrix with owner, evidence, status, and dependency.

---

## 4. Authority Interface Framework
Map receiving/approving authority by asset and obligation type:
- Planning and land obligations
- Fire acceptance
- Municipal infrastructure handover
- Environmental and quality supervision interfaces

Key handover evidence package:
1. As-built drawings and survey records.
2. Test reports and commissioning records.
3. Completion photos with location references.
4. Defect rectification log and closeout confirmation.
5. Maintenance responsibility statement and handover minutes.

---

## 5. End-to-End Process (Practical Sequence)
1. Extract all acceptance and filing critical obligations from approvals and contract conditions.
2. Build closeout register with responsible party and target date.
3. Pre-align with relevant receiving authorities on handover/evidence format.
4. Complete works and compile evidence pack by condition.
5. Run internal readiness check before formal submission.
6. Submit acceptance/filing package and manage authority comments with tracked responses.
7. Complete outstanding handover actions and secure confirmation records.
8. Close all conditions, issue final closeout confirmation, and align with commercial handover timeline.

---

## 6. Submission & Document Control Standard
Minimum recommended package structure:
- Section A: Grant-condition tracker and status summary.
- Section B: Drawings and approvals set.
- Section C: Completion records and test/inspection evidence.
- Section D: Authority handover records (department-wise).
- Section E: Correspondence log, meeting minutes, and action tracker.

Discipline rules:
- One source of truth register for condition status.
- Version-controlled evidence index with file references.
- Written confirmation for every verbal authority direction.
- Weekly closeout meeting with action owners until CC/consent milestone is achieved.

---

## 7. Risk Hotspots
- Late discovery of unclosed approval obligations.
- Mismatch between built condition and accepted scope.
- Fragmented ownership across consultant, contractor, and legal teams.
- Public-interface handover scope disagreements at late stage.
- Missing test records or non-traceable as-built documentation.
- Assuming filing timing without confirmed authority feedback cycle.

---

## 8. Mitigation Checklist
- Closeout-condition matrix prepared and approved by project leadership.
- Receiving authority map confirmed early.
- Handover evidence template agreed before completion stage.
- Department interface log maintained with dated decisions.
- Closeout dry-run conducted before formal acceptance/final filing submission.
- Legal and commercial teams aligned on fallback sequencing if one authority stream is delayed.

---

## 9. Practical Output Checklist
- Completion and filing strategy note issued.
- Condition-by-condition closeout register live and owner-assigned.
- Authority handover package completed and traceable.
- Department comments tracker closed or actioned with timetable.
- Assignment critical path integrated with legal/commercial milestones.
- Residual obligations clearly listed for post-consent governance.

---

*Project teams should align this framework with project-specific approval conditions, local authority correspondence, and legal advice for transaction-critical decisions.*
