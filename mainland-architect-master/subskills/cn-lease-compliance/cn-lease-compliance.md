# Mainland Land-Condition Compliance
Covers the relationship between land-transfer obligations and design/delivery approvals in Mainland projects.

---

## 1. Three-Tier Compliance Framework (Mainland)
In Mainland delivery, align these regimes together:
1. **Land-transfer obligations:** 出让条件 + 土地出让合同条款.
2. **Planning controls:** 控规 and planning permit constraints.
3. **Technical code compliance:** national and local standards for design and construction.

Key point: indicator definitions and measurement scope may differ between planning approvals, land contract text, and technical codes; reconcile explicitly.

---

## 2. Typical Obligation-Adjustment Paths

| Mechanism | Purpose | Process |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Land-condition adjustment** | Permanent or phased change to land-use obligations/indicators. | Coordinate natural-resources authority and contractual procedures. |
| **Planning-condition optimization** | Align project reality with planning control requirements. | Submit technical justification and revised compliance package. |
| **Special approval pathway** | Resolve non-standard or legacy conditions. | Authority-led case-by-case review and formal record closure. |

---

## 3. Common Constraint-Conflict Matrix

| Feature | Land Contract Perspective | Planning/Code Perspective |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Building area** | Contractual area/indicator limits may be explicit | Calculated per standards and planning methods |
| **Height control** | Contract may specify hard cap obligations | Planning and skyline/aviation controls apply |
| **Use function** | Contractual land-use clauses | Planning permits and code-compliance by function |
| **Municipal obligations** | Infrastructure/public-space obligations possible | Technical and acceptance routes differ by authority |
| **Phasing conditions** | Contract may lock phasing or delivery obligations | Construction/programme may require adjustments |

---

## 4. Compliance Workflow
1. Extract all land-contract obligations into a closeout matrix.
2. Cross-check with planning permit indicators and design assumptions.
3. Flag conflicts early (area, height, use, phasing, municipal obligations).
4. Prepare adjustment/clarification submissions where needed.
5. Maintain authority comment tracker through construction and completion.

---

## 5. Technical Coordination Checklist

- Indicator definitions are unified across contract/planning/code submissions.
- `用地红线` and planning control boundaries are consistent in all drawing sets.
- Any non-standard contract obligations are mapped to responsible disciplines.
- Change instructions are checked against land and planning constraints before issue.

---

## 6. High-Risk Failure Modes

| Risk | Consequence | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Contract obligations discovered late | Major redesign or commercial impact | Build and maintain contract-obligation matrix from day one |
| Planning and contract metrics inconsistent | Authority pushback and delay | Single approved indicator source and controlled updates |
| Phasing ignores contract dependencies | Delivery and permit bottlenecks | Add contract dependencies into baseline programme |
| Local authority interpretation not captured | Repeated submission cycles | Record every authority clarification in writing and update matrix |

---

## 7. Required Inputs (ask if missing)

- City and district
- Latest land-transfer contract/conditions summary
- Current planning approval status
- Stage (`方案` / `初设` / `施工图` / `施工`)
- Any authority clarification letters already received
