# Mainland Design Theory

Design theory applied to Mainland China’s diverse climates, governance constraints, and delivery realities.

---

## 1. Critical Regionalism (Mainland application)

Critical regionalism is practical in China because climate, craft supply chains, and planning controls differ by region.

| Principle | Mainland Application |
|---|---|
| Tectonic expression | Make structure + envelope logic legible without decorative “skin” |
| Climate response | North–south differences matter; climate-specific shading, insulation, ventilation strategies |
| Local material | Respect local supply chains and durability realities; avoid “import-only” details that can’t be maintained |
| Topographic engagement | Site section, view, wind, and logistics shape feasibility as much as concept |

---

## 2. Density and Mixed-use Urbanism (China)

High-intensity mixed-use is common in tier-1/2 cities, but it must be reconciled with:
- planning indicators (FAR/coverage/height)
- evacuation/fire compartment logic
- transit and delivery logistics

- Programme stacking: residential, retail, office, carpark, MTR concourse in a single section
- Elevator as urban street: vertical circulation replaces horizontal movement
- Podium as urban ground: artificial ground plane at L3–L5 creates new public realm
- Composite building as urban block: single building performs the role of an entire city block

---

## 3. Practice-facing design principles (what survives审图/造价/工期)

- **Section-first feasibility**: vertical stacking, transfers, refuge/plant logic, and logistics early.
- **Approval-aware clarity**: design intent should be explainable in authority and审图 language.
- **Cost and procurement realism**: details that cannot be built repeatedly will not survive fast-track.

---

## 4. Heritage / Adaptive Reuse Theory (Mainland)

| Concept | Mainland Application |
|---|---|
| Significance hierarchy | Decide what is non-negotiable (CDEs) before layout work |
| Minimal intervention | Avoid irreversible damage; make additions legible and maintainable |
| Compliance trade-offs | Fire/accessibility constraints must be resolved with pragmatic packages |
| Evidence discipline | Document before/during/after; method statements matter |

---

## 5. Sustainability Philosophy (双碳 / 绿色建筑)

- Green building is not a “label chase” — integrate early decisions (orientation, envelope, systems).
- Whole-life thinking: operational + embodied carbon + user wellbeing.
- Treat green/low-carbon deliverables as programme-critical, not late add-ons.

---

## 6. Compositional Principles (high-density China)

| Principle | Application |
|---|---|
| Section as generator | Podium/tower, transfer, refuge, plant, and logistics dominate feasibility |
| Threshold design | Public → semi-public → private must work with management and security models |
| View + daylight | Daylight and view value shape unit/product strategy |
| Ground activation | Retail frontage and pedestrian system are business-critical in mixed-use |

---

## 7. Required Inputs (ask if missing)

- City and target client/audience (SOE developer / private / gov)
- Building type and delivery mode
- Any heritage designation constraints
- Sustainability target (e.g., 绿色建筑星级 / LEED)
