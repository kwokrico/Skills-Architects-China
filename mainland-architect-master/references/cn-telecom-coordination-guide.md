# Telecom Coordination Guide — Mainland

Reference for `cn-telecom-coordination`.

---

## Carriers

- 中国移动、中国联通、中国电信（及广电等）— 地下管线权属分散。
- 开挖前须 **查明管线**（物探+开挖验证）。

---

## Workflow

1. **Request as-built** from carriers and municipal archive.
2. **Site survey** and paint/mark protection zone.
3. **Protection agreement** signed before excavation within buffer.
4. **Licensed contractor** for any touch/move of carrier plant.
5. **Witness and sign-off** before backfill.

---

## Excavation Near Plant

| Distance | Action |
|----------|--------|
| Within protection zone | Stop; carrier on site |
| Parallel trench | Hand dig verification |
| Crane swing | Overhead line clearance per carrier |

---

## Risk

- Unrecorded fibre cuts → outage liability + delay.
- Coordinate with `cn-site-establishment` PCI pack.

---

## Outputs

- Telecom protection plan drawing
- Carrier contact log
- Diversion as-built before cover
