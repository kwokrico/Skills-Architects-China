# Construction Stakeholder Register (Mainland)

Coordination map for architects. Names of bureaus differ by city. Confirm the local name before a submission. This register does not list personal contacts.

| Role | Typical body | Architect uses them for | Not their job |
|------|----------------|-------------------------|---------------|
| Planning and natural resources | 自然资源局 / 规自局 | 控规, red lines, planning permit, 规划核实 | Construction quality |
| Housing and construction | 住建局 | 施工许可, quality and safety supervision, 审图 system | Land-use change |
| Drawing review | 审图机构 | 施工图审查 and comment closure | Planning permit |
| Fire | 消防救援 / the fire design-review window under the current rules | 特殊建设工程 fire design review and fire acceptance; other projects often use filing and sampling | Contract practical completion |
| Traffic police | 交警 | Construction traffic and some 占道 approvals | Building fire strategy |
| Transport / municipal roads | 交通局 / 市政 | Road opening, reinstatement, bus stops | Tenant fit-out |
| Urban management | 城管 | Street occupation and site appearance where the city assigns it | Design approval |
| Cultural relics | 文物局 | Protected sites, protection zones, archaeology | Ordinary facade colour unless the control plan says so |
| Ecology and environment | 生态环境局 | EIA category and construction dust/noise limits | Green-building star certificates |
| Civil aviation | 民航 / the airport obstacle administrator | Height near an airport | Ordinary 控规 height on a site outside the surface |
| Water | 水务 / 供水 / 排水 | Supply, drainage, and flood constraints | Telecom ducts |
| Power and gas | 供电 / 燃气 | Diversions and incoming capacity | Architectural layout |
| Telecom carriers | 移动 / 电信 / 联通 | Entry ducts and equipment rooms | Road-occupation permits |
| Metro or railway | Operator protection office | Works in a protection zone | General traffic assessment |
| Special equipment | 市场监管 | Lift and escalator registration and inspection | Architectural stair design |
| Supervision | 监理单位 | Statutory site supervision | Design authorship |
| Contractor | 施工单位 | Means, methods, and site safety | 审图 seal |
| Cost consultant | 造价咨询 | Measurement and valuation | Design responsibility |
| Client | 建设单位 | Decisions, land, and permits in the client's name | Design seal |

## How to use

1. Copy the rows that apply.
2. Add the city's bureau name and the application window.
3. Mark each permit as not started, submitted, or received.
4. Do not invent a telephone number or an officer's name.

Related skills: `cn-site-establishment`, `cn-consent-scheduling`, `cn-traffic-coordination`, `cn-telecom-coordination`.
