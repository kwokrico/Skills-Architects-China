# Traffic Submission Types — Mainland

Reference for `cn-traffic-coordination`.

---

## Planning Stage

| Submission | Trigger | Authority (typical) | Output |
|------------|---------|---------------------|--------|
| 交通影响评价 (TIA) | 控规/地方阈值、大型项目 | 规自/交警/交通 | 报告+改善措施 |
| 停车位配建复核 | 报建 | 规自 | 指标符合证明 |

---

## Construction Stage

| Submission | Trigger | Authority (typical) | Output |
|------------|---------|---------------------|--------|
| 施工交通组织方案 | 占道、开挖、材料运输 | 交警 | 批复意见 |
| 交通疏导方案 | 封闭车道、改道 | 交警+城管 | 临时交通标志方案 |
| 夜间施工交通 | 超限运输、夜间浇筑 | 交警 | 时段许可 |

---

## Do Not Conflate

- **TIA** answers "does the development trip generation work?" (planning).
- **施工交通组织** answers "how does construction operate on the road?" (construction).

---

## Programme Tips

- Allow 2–4 weeks review for 交警意见 (city-dependent).
- Link hoarding drawings to TMP figures.
