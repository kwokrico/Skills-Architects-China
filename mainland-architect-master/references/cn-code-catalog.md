## City hook

A local conclusion needs the current instrument for that city. The six cities the suite already routes are Beijing, Shanghai, Shenzhen, Guangzhou, Chengdu, and Hangzhou. Their planning technical rules are named, without numbers, in [City_Planning_Technical_Rules_CS.md](../../cn_s_reference/MNR/summaries/City_Planning_Technical_Rules_CS.md) and [cn-statutory-source-register.md](cn-statutory-source-register.md).

| City or region | Series to ask for |
|---|---|
| Beijing | The 2003 planning design rules (通则) still cited by the 2020 commission notice, plus the current DB11 edition |
| Shanghai | The land-use and building-management technical rules (Order No. 12 / No. 52), plus the current DGJ08 edition |
| Shenzhen | 深圳市城市规划标准与准则 (2025 reissue), plus the current SJG edition |
| Guangzhou | 广州市城乡规划技术规定 (2019 third revision), plus the current Guangdong DBJ edition |
| Chengdu | 成都市城市规划管理技术规定（2024）, 成府函〔2024〕41号. Do not apply an undated consultation supplement |
| Hangzhou | 杭州市城市规划管理技术规定 issued 2026-02-05, plus the current local DB edition |
| Other cities | The current DB, DGJ, or DBJ edition named in the planning permit or 审图意见 |

National baseline still applies when the city edition is missing. Say so. Do not reuse a number from another city.
