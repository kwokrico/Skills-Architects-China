# Common Material Modules (Mainland Coordination)

Modules for setting-out studies. Product standards and the project specification govern. Do not treat a module as a code minimum.

| System | Coordination module | Note |
|--------|---------------------|------|
| Fired clay brick | 240 × 115 × 53 mm unit, often set out with a 10 mm joint so the planning module is 125 mm in plan | Confirm the product standard and whether the wall is a facing skin or a structural wall. Structural masonry follows the structural general code, not this table. |
| Concrete block | Project-specific (commonly 390 or 190 mm lengths) | Confirm the local product before drawing a module grid |
| Prefabricated residential | Often a 300 mm planning module for room and component sizes | Confirm the city's prefabrication guide and the factory's actual moulds (`cn-mic-dfma`) |
| Floor-to-floor | Residential and office defaults are in `cn-architect-foundations` | Structure and services zones can break a neat module |
| Curtain wall | Mullion spacing is a design and cost choice; 1.2–1.5 m is only a starting sketch | Wind load, glass size, and the manufacturer's system control the real spacing (`cn-building-envelope`) |
| Ceiling and raised floor | 600 mm grid is a common services module | Confirm tile, luminaire, and floor-box sizes |
| Waterproof upstand | Do not invent a millimetre height | GB 55030-2022 and the detail standard |
| Door leaf | Coordinate with the access clear width, which is not the leaf size | `cn-human-scale-dimensions.md` |

## Rules

- Dimension to finished face or to structure, and say which.
