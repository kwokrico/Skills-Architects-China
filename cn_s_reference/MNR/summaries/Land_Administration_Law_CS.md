# Land Administration Law (土地管理法)

**Architect critical summary for schematic design**

1986 law, revised 1998, third amendment adopted 2019-08-26 and in force 2020-01-01. | National People's Congress; natural-resources authorities administer land

> Scope note: This is an architect's digest for routing and early design. It is not the text of the law or standard. Confirm every number, clause, and edition in the official publication before design, tender, or submission.

## Regulatory Overview

Legal basis for who may use land, for what purpose, and on what grant or allocation. A design that ignores the granted use is not a planning-variance sketch. It is a land-law problem.

## Critical topics

- Urban state-owned construction land and collective land are not interchangeable. A rural site is not designed as if a grant contract already exists.
- Use, term, and development conditions in the grant contract have to match the scheme. Plot ratio in a brochure is not the contract.
- Farmland protection and land-use control sit here. Do not treat a greenfield housing layout as approved because a masterplan drawing shows it.
- Idle granted land for real-estate development is handled with the Urban Real Estate Administration Law, not by redesigning the massing to "use it up".
- The planning permit is a separate document under the Urban and Rural Planning Law. This law does not issue that permit.

## Schematic-design takeaway

Ask for the grant contract or allocation decision before locking use and intensity. Do not quote an idle-land fee from this file.
