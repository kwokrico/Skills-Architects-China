# City planning technical rules — six practice cities

**Architect critical summary for schematic design**

Title-level index for the cities the suite already routes. No plot ratio, sunlight hour, parking ratio, or setback is copied here. | Municipal planning authorities

> Scope note: This is an architect's digest for routing and early design. It is not the text of the law or standard. Confirm every number, clause, and edition in the official publication before design, tender, or submission.

## Regulatory Overview

National GB/T 50353 measures construction area. It does not decide what counts toward plot ratio (容积率) in a city. For Beijing, Shanghai, Shenzhen, Guangzhou, Chengdu, and Hangzhou, open the instrument named below, then the project regulatory plan (控规) and land-grant conditions (出让条件). Do not borrow a number from another city.

Local building standards (DB, DGJ, DBJ, SJG) are not in this digest. Ask for the edition on the drawing-review opinion.

## Critical topics

- Beijing: 北京地区建设工程规划设计通则（试行）, 市规发〔2003〕514号. On 2020-07-27 the municipal planning and natural-resources commission said it remains in force, except where a later business-environment or approval-reform document governs.
- Shanghai: 上海市城市规划管理技术规定（土地使用、建筑管理）, Government Order No. 12 (2003), amended and reissued under Order No. 52 (2010). Capacity, spacing, setback, height, greenery, and parking are in this one document. An application interpretation was reissued on 2023-09-26.
- Shenzhen: 深圳市城市规划标准与准则（修订汇总版）, notice dated 2025-11-03. Density, plot ratio, and parking are chapters of this document, not a separate national code.
- Guangzhou: 广州市城乡规划技术规定, Government Order No. 71 (2012), third revision Order No. 168 (2019).
- Chengdu: 成都市城市规划管理技术规定（2024）, approved by 成府函〔2024〕41号 on 2024-06-15. Obtain it from the Chengdu planning bureau. A later supplement circulated as a consultation draft with no effective date. Do not apply that draft.
- Hangzhou: 杭州市城市规划管理技术规定, issued 2026-02-05 by the Hangzhou planning and natural-resources bureau. It applies to the urban built-up area. Counties may use it as a reference. The gazette contains control tables. This digest does not copy them.
- Green-building rules, prefabrication-rate policy, and local fire-review rules for these cities were not identified as one current document. Ask the housing bureau and the fire-review window.
- The land-grant contract, planning conditions, red-line drawing, and review opinions are project documents. They are not replaced by this index.

## Schematic-design takeaway

Name the city, open its technical rules, and still read the site's own control plan. A missing city stays on the national baseline only.
