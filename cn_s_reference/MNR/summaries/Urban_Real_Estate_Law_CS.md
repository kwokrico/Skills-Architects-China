# Urban Real Estate Administration Law (城市房地产管理法)

**Architect critical summary for schematic design**

1994 law, third amendment adopted 2019-08-26 and in force 2020-01-01. | National People's Congress

> Scope note: This is an architect's digest for routing and early design. It is not the text of the law or standard. Confirm every number, clause, and edition in the official publication before design, tender, or submission.

## Regulatory Overview

Covers development on state-owned land inside a planned urban area: grant and allocation of the use right, development, and later transfer, mortgage, and lease. It is not the design code and it is not the planning permit.

## Critical topics

- A grant is time-limited and paid. An allocation is a different right, used for the public purposes the law lists, and is not a silent substitute for a grant.
- The grant has to follow the land-use plan, the urban plan, and the annual construction-land plan. The architect reads the granted use, term, and conditions. The architect does not draft the grant.
- Development on granted land has to follow the contract's use and start-of-work terms. Changing the use to make the massing fit is a contract change, not a design option.
- Registration of the land right and of the completed building are separate later acts. A planning permit does not register title.

## Schematic-design takeaway

Read the grant or allocation with the planning conditions. Do not quote a contract penalty from this file.
