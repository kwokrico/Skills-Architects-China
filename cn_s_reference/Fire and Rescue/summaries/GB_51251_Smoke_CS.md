# GB 51251 Smoke-control technical standard (建筑防烟排烟系统技术标准)

**Architect critical summary for schematic design**

GB 51251-2017《建筑防烟排烟系统技术标准》. Confirm the implementation date in the official announcement. | Ministry of Housing and Urban-Rural Development

> Scope note: This is an architect's digest for routing and early design. It is not the text of the law or standard. Confirm every number, clause, and edition in the official publication before design, tender, or submission.

## Regulatory Overview

Detailed standard for smoke control and smoke exhaust. GB 55037-2022 is the mandatory fire floor. GB 55036-2022 is the mandatory floor for fire facilities. Where GB 51251 conflicts with either general code, the general code prevails. A smoke-calculation printout is not a fire-acceptance opinion.

## Critical topics

- Decide whether the scheme uses natural smoke exhaust, mechanical smoke exhaust, or both, and record that choice before freezing the core and the plant rooms.
- Smoke reservoirs, vents, and fan rooms need architectural space. Do not leave them for the MEP drawings alone.
- Do not quote a smoke-travel distance, a vent area, or a fan capacity from memory. Open the official table or mark the number unverified.
- Local fire-review guides may be stricter. Ask for the city.

## Schematic-design takeaway

Name the smoke strategy and the standard edition. Do not claim the system complies until the clause and the city supplement are both checked.
