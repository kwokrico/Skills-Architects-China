# Interim Provisions on Fire Design Review and Acceptance

**Architect critical summary for schematic design**

MOHURD Order No. 51, amended by Order No. 58, in force 2023-10-30. | Ministry of Housing and Urban-Rural Development

> Scope note: This is an architect's digest for routing and early design. It is not the text of the law or standard. Confirm every number, clause, and edition in the official publication before design, tender, or submission.

## Regulatory Overview

Special construction projects need fire design review and fire acceptance. Other projects use completion filing and classified sampling. Provinces publish the catalogue that splits ordinary and key projects.

## Critical topics

- Authority check (2026-11-07): Article 3 assigns construction fire review, acceptance, filing and spot checks to local housing-and-construction authorities according to their responsibilities. Confirm the municipal service window.
- This is separate from public-gathering premises' pre-use/opening fire-safety checks by fire-rescue institutions under Fire Protection Law Article 15.

- Identify whether the project is a 特殊建设工程 before choosing the path. The order lists the categories; do not guess from height alone without the list.
- Special fire design (no national standard, new material, or heritage constraint) needs the extra technical file the order describes, which can include analysis and, for major or high-hazard cases, physical tests.
- Other projects file after completion. A complete filing receives a voucher; it is not the same document as a special-project acceptance opinion.
- Sampling failure has a rectification path. Do not advise the user to skip filing.

## Schematic-design takeaway

Split the programme into special-project review/acceptance or other-project filing. Say which one applies.
