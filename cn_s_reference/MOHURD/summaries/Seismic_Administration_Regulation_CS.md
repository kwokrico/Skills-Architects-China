# Construction seismic administration regulation (建设工程抗震管理条例)

**Architect critical summary for schematic design**

State Council Order No. 744, issued 2021-07-19, in force 2021-09-01. | State Council; housing authorities supervise building works

> Scope note: This is an architect's digest for routing and early design. It is not the text of the law or standard. Confirm every number, clause, and edition in the official publication before design, tender, or submission.

## Regulatory Overview

This is the legal duty behind seismic design, identification, and strengthening. It is not a member-size code. Intensity and detailing still come from the seismic standards, which are not digested here.

## Critical topics

- New, extended, and altered construction has to meet the mandatory seismic standards. The client must not instruct a reduction.
- Survey documents have to state the site category and the seismic effect of the ground. Design documents have to state the seismic measures. Isolation or energy-dissipation devices need explicit performance, inspection, and maintenance requirements.
- Schools, kindergartens, hospitals, elder-care, child-welfare, and emergency-command buildings are priority defence. Confirm the current classification before calling a building ordinary.
- Over-code high-rise work has a special seismic review. Do not treat a concept sketch as that review.
- Existing buildings that lack the required seismic capacity are an identification and strengthening problem, not a fit-out note.
- Rural housing is in scope. Do not apply an urban high-rise rule by habit, and do not ignore the rural duty either.

## Schematic-design takeaway

Ask for the intensity and whether the building is priority defence or over-code before freezing the structural concept. Do not quote a drift limit from this file.
