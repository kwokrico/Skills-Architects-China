# GB 50009 Load code for the design of building structures (建筑结构荷载规范)

**Architect critical summary for schematic design**

GB 50009-2012《建筑结构荷载规范》. Confirm the implementation date and any partial revision in the official announcement. | Ministry of Housing and Urban-Rural Development

> Scope note: This is an architect's digest for routing and early design. It is not the text of the law or standard. Confirm every number, clause, and edition in the official publication before design, tender, or submission.

## Regulatory Overview

This code names the actions the structural engineer combines: permanent actions, imposed floor and roof loads, wind, snow, and the other variable actions it lists. Seismic action is GB 50011, not a table in this code. Member sizes are a structural calculation. They are not read off a span table in an architecture skill.

## Critical topics

- Ask for the city before quoting a basic wind pressure or a snow load. Both are lookups in the official tables.
- Architectural choices that change load — green roofs, rooftop plant, heavy partitions, crowd rooms — go into the load brief. They do not get sized here.
- Do not import a Hong Kong or US wind figure.
- Foundation and member design stay with `cn-structural-systems` and the structural engineer.

## Schematic-design takeaway

Record which actions apply and who calculates them. Do not publish a beam or column size from this digest.
