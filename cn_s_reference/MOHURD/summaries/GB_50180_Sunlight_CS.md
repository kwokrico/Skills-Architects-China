# GB 50180 Urban residential planning standard (城市居住区规划设计标准)

**Architect critical summary for schematic design**

GB 50180-2018《城市居住区规划设计标准》. Confirm the implementation date and any local amendment in the official announcement. | Ministry of Housing and Urban-Rural Development

> Scope note: This is an architect's digest for routing and early design. It is not the text of the law or standard. Confirm every number, clause, and edition in the official publication before design, tender, or submission.

## Regulatory Overview

National method for residential district layout, including sunlight spacing (日照). Cities often set a stricter hour requirement and a specified calculation day in the 控规 or a municipal rule. Those local rules can tighten the national method. They are not a room-daylight calculation.

## Critical topics

- Residential applicability check: GB 55038-2025 took effect on 2025-05-01 as a mandatory residential project code. Check its applicable requirements before relying on GB 50180 or older residential provisions; preserve the distinction between sunlight and room daylight. The announcement also repeals GB 50033-2013 clause 4.0.1. Do not silently reuse that clause. For existing approvals, verify local transition arrangements and approval dates rather than assuming either blanket exemption or retrospective redesign.

- 日照 answers whether a residential window receives the required sun. 采光 (GB 55016, and GB 50033 where it does not conflict) answers whether a room is lit. Do not swap the two.
- Do not quote a sunlight hour or a spacing coefficient from memory. Open the official table and the city rule, or mark the number unverified.
- Missing city: describe the national method only, and halt on a spacing number.
- The massing test is a sunlight envelope. It is an input to `cn-concept-design`, not a pass from this digest alone.

## Schematic-design takeaway

Ask for the city sunlight rule before freezing residential massing.
