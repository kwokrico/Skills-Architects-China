# GB 50352 Standard for Design of Civil Buildings (民用建筑设计统一标准)

**Architect critical summary for schematic design**

The edition widely used in practice is GB 50352-2019. Confirm on the national standards catalogue before submission. | Ministry of Housing and Urban-Rural Development

> Scope note: This is an architect's digest for routing and early design. It is not the text of the law or standard. Confirm every number, clause, and edition in the official publication before design, tender, or submission.

## Regulatory Overview

Recommended unified design standard for civil buildings. It remains useful for topics the general code does not exhaust, but it does not override GB 55031.

## Critical topics

- Use it after GB 55031, not instead of GB 55031.
- Typology standards (residential, school, hospital, office) still add their own rules.
- Do not treat a GB 50352 figure as mandatory if GB 55031 states a different requirement.

## Schematic-design takeaway

Cite GB 55031 as the mandatory floor and GB 50352 as the detailed standard where it does not conflict.
