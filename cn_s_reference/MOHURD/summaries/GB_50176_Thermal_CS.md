# GB 50176 Thermal design code for civil buildings (民用建筑热工设计规范)

**Architect critical summary for schematic design**

GB 50176-2016《民用建筑热工设计规范》. Confirm the implementation date and any partial revision in the official announcement. | Ministry of Housing and Urban-Rural Development

> Scope note: This is an architect's digest for routing and early design. It is not the text of the law or standard. Confirm every number, clause, and edition in the official publication before design, tender, or submission.

## Regulatory Overview

This code divides civil-building climate into five zones: 严寒, 寒冷, 夏热冬冷, 夏热冬暖, and 温和. The zone selects the detailed residential energy standard. It does not replace GB 55015-2021. Energy limits that conflict with GB 55015 follow GB 55015.

## Critical topics

- State the project city and its climate zone before recommending insulation, glazing, or shading.
- Severe-cold and cold zones point at JGJ 26. Hot-summer cold-winter points at JGJ 134. Hot-summer warm-winter points at JGJ 75. Confirm each edition. The mild zone still needs the official table, not a neighbouring city's limits.
- Do not copy a U-value from another zone or from a Hong Kong OTTV figure.
- Indoor thermal environment also sits under GB 55016-2021 where that general code applies.
