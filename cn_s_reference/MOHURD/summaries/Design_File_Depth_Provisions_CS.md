# Design file depth provisions (建筑工程设计文件编制深度规定, 2016)

**Architect critical summary for schematic design**

建质函〔2016〕247号, in force 2017-01-01. Replaces the 2008 edition. | Ministry of Housing and Urban-Rural Development

> Scope note: This is an architect's digest for routing and early design. It is not the text of the law or standard. Confirm every number, clause, and edition in the official publication before design, tender, or submission.

## Regulatory Overview

This is the national depth document for scheme design (方案设计), preliminary design (初步设计), and construction drawings (施工图设计). It is not a planning code and it is not a substitute for a city submission template.

## Critical topics

- Scheme drawings have to be deep enough for approval and for the next design stage. A competition board is not this document.
- Preliminary design has to be deep enough to start construction drawings and to pass preliminary approval where the authority requires that stage.
- Construction drawings have to be deep enough to buy materials, fabricate non-standard items, and build.
- A technically simple civil building may go from approved scheme to construction drawings when the authority does not require preliminary design and the contract does not either.
- The 2016 edition adds depth for green-building measures, prefabrication, energy calculations, over-code structural feasibility, curtain wall, excavation support, and intelligent systems. Those sections are not copied here.
- Local drawing-review templates can ask for more. They do not drop a mandatory drawing this document requires.

## Schematic-design takeaway

Name the stage, then open this document for the drawing list. Do not invent a sheet from memory.
