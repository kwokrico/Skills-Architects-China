# Measures for general contracting of building and municipal works (房屋建筑和市政基础设施项目工程总承包管理办法)

**Architect critical summary for schematic design**

建市规〔2019〕12号, issued 2019-12-23, in force 2020-03-01. | Ministry of Housing and Urban-Rural Development and National Development and Reform Commission

> Scope note: This is an architect's digest for routing and early design. It is not the text of the law or standard. Confirm every number, clause, and edition in the official publication before design, tender, or submission.

## Regulatory Overview

Engineering, procurement, and construction (工程总承包) means one contractor takes design plus procurement and construction, or design plus construction, and is responsible for quality, safety, time, and cost inside that scope. These measures do not choose the route for a project. The contract does.

## Critical topics

- Do not call a traditional build-only contract an engineering, procurement, and construction contract.
- Risk that the measures leave to the parties includes price movement, law changes, unforeseen ground, client-caused delay, and force majeure. The particular conditions have to say how those are shared. This digest does not allocate them.
- The contract has to say who submits construction drawings for review and who carries comment-cycle delay.
- A model contract (示范文本) is not these measures. GF-2020-0216 is not ingested. Do not quote a model clause from memory.
- Tendering law still applies where a tender is required. These measures do not repeal it.

## Schematic-design takeaway

Name the route as unselected until the contract family is chosen. Then read these measures and the signed form together.
