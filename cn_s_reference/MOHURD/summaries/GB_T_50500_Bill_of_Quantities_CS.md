## Regulatory Overview

This is a measurement and pricing standard for bills of quantities. It is not an architectural drawing standard. No measurement rule, unit rate, or quantity method is recorded in this file.

## Critical topics

- Do not say a bill complies with GB/T 50500 unless the cost consultant names the edition and the contract adopts it.
- The architect checks that the bill covers the drawn scope and the specification. The cost consultant owns the quantities.
- A local pricing rule can sit beside this standard. Ask which one the contract uses.
- Removing mandatory fire or access scope to lower a control price is not a measurement choice.

## Schematic-design takeaway

Name the measurement standard as unknown until the contract or the cost consultant states the edition.
