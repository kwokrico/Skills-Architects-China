# Mainland Small Works / Fit-out Compliance (装修装饰/小微工程)

Mainland does not have a single national “MWCS” equivalent. Small works compliance is managed through a mix of property management rules, relevant technical codes, and city-specific filing/permit/acceptance requirements.

Regulatory hierarchy (for code-facing items):
- National baseline first: `GB / GB-T / JGJ`
- Then local supplements: `DB / DGJ / DBJ` (city/province)

If city is not specified, treat guidance as national baseline pre-check and request local supplement confirmation.

---

## 1. Scope Triage (what kind of “small works” is it?)
Classify by regulatory impact rather than HK-style classes:

- **Interior non-structural fit-out**: partitions, finishes, MEP re-routing without affecting fire compartments/egress.
- **Works affecting fire integrity**: fire doors, fire-rated walls, smoke control interfaces, alarm coverage.
- **Works affecting structure**: slab opening, load-bearing wall, heavy plant, facade anchoring.
- **Works affecting facade / public safety**: signboards, external cladding, canopies.
- **Change of use**: occupancy/function changes that trigger code re-check and acceptance.

Outcome: determine whether you need (a) design sign-off, (b) drawing review, (c) construction permit impact, (d) acceptance impact.

---

## 2. High-risk “small works” that often get teams into trouble
- Slab openings / new stairs / mezzanines
- Altering fire compartments, refuge spaces, or protected routes
- Removing/chasing structural members or heavy anchor loads
- Changing facade materials (combustibility, fire stopping)
- Adding kitchens / increased fire load / gas interfaces
- Tenant fit-out that changes occupant load materially

---

## 3. Practical Compliance Workflow (recommended)

1. **Scope freeze**: confirm tenant brief and boundaries (common parts vs private).
2. **Red-line checks**: confirm no change to land/planning indicators.
3. **Fire/egress integrity**: treat `GB 50016` as baseline; do not degrade protected routes.
4. **Structural safety**: any structural touch must be engineered and approved by responsible parties.
5. **Property/IO process**: management office approvals and method statements (especially for facade/structure).
6. **Evidence governance**: as-built set + commissioning records, ready for audits/acceptance where required.

---

## 4. Technical Guidelines & Compliance
* **Area/accountability:** do not create illegal area; building area is calculated per `GB/T 50353` baseline logic.
* **Fire safety:** maintain fire compartments and means of egress per `GB 50016` baseline; verify local supplements.
* **Non-compliance risk:** “small works” executed without required approvals can become `未批先建/擅自变更/违建` depending on scope.
* **City variance:** filing/permit/acceptance requirements differ materially; always ask for `city_context`.

## 6. Required Inputs (ask if missing)

- City and district
- Building type and occupancy (office/retail/hotel/etc.)
- Whether structure/facade/fire compartments are affected
- Whether change of use is involved
- Target date and whether acceptance constraints exist
