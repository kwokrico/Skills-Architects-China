# Mainland Approvals & Scheduling
Covers realistic Mainland project scheduling around 方案/初设/施工图, 施工图审查, 施工许可证, 消防验收, and 竣工联合验收/备案.

---

## 1. Scheduling Principles (Mainland reality)

- Do not promise uniform “statutory” response times across cities.
- Assume multi-agency bottlenecks and iteration cycles (审图意见闭环 is the common critical path).
- Fast-track works only when scope boundary and dependency chain are explicit.
- Always capture `city_context` and current platform workflow (`一网通办` variations).

---

## 2. Typical Mainland Critical Path (baseline)

1. `方案设计` freeze (indicator compliance + red-line alignment).
2. `初步设计` freeze (systems route + investment confidence).
3. `施工图设计` issue (coordinated set).
4. `施工图审查` close loop (comments resolved and stamped/accepted).
5. `施工许可证` readiness (permit prerequisites met).
6. Construction start and `施工配合`.

Key risk: if `审图` comments trigger redesign, downstream permit and procurement are hit.

---

## 3. Fast-Track Tactics (use selectively)

- Parallelize only what is truly independent:
  - Early geotech + foundation option study (informing structural route).
  - Early fire strategy workshops to avoid late-stage route changes.
  - Early envelope/system procurement to lock long-lead items.
- Use “pre-consult” logic: request authority/审图机构 informal feedback before formal submission when schedule is tight.

---

## 4. Practical Programme Outputs

Minimum deliverables:
- Critical path map with decision gates by stage
- “审图闭环” tracker with owners and due dates
- Permit dependency list (planning →审图→施工许可)
- Float policy: buffers around review rework cycles

## 5. Required Inputs (ask if missing)

- City and district
- Current stage and target site-start date
- Delivery mode (traditional vs EPC)
- Known authority/审图机构 constraints
- Any already-issued planning/审图 comments
