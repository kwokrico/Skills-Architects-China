## Planning submission

1. **《中华人民共和国城乡规划法》**
   This is the primary basis for planning approvals. For construction in urban or town planning areas, the developer applies for a **建设工程规划许可证** (Construction Project Planning Permit, “工程规划许可证”); the statutory application includes land-use proof and the construction design proposal, plus a detailed site plan where required. [mee.gov](https://www.mee.gov.cn/ywgz/fgbz/fl/201906/t20190605_705768.shtml)

2. **Planning conditions and controlling plan**
   Acknowledge the applicable **控制性详细规划** (regulatory detailed plan), **规划条件** (planning conditions), land parcel boundary/red-line, land-use right document, and the **国有建设用地使用权出让合同** where land is granted. These establish permitted use, development intensity, height, setbacks, FAR/plot ratio, site coverage, parking, public facilities, and other parcel controls. [dnr.yn.gov](https://dnr.yn.gov.cn/html/2023/liuyanhuifu_1128/43891.html)

3. **Project approval basis**
   Include the applicable **approval,核准, or备案** document for the investment project, together with the land-use documentation and planning/design proposal required by the local natural-resources and planning authority. A typical municipal permit submission also calls for qualified design drawings and a site plan. [english.beijing.gov](https://english.beijing.gov.cn/investinginbeijing/Start_Your_Business/project_construction/construction_permit/202005/t20200525_1908384.html)

4. **Specialist approval opinions**
   Require a local check for parallel or prerequisite specialist opinions, such as civil-air-defence (**人防**), landscape/green-space, transport, heritage, environmental impact assessment, utilities, and airport/height-control constraints. Which ones are mandatory depends on the city and project type. [english.beijing.gov](https://english.beijing.gov.cn/investinginbeijing/Start_Your_Business/project_construction/construction_permit/202005/t20200525_1908384.html)

When drafting a `skill.md` file for architectural planning submissions and drawing/building regulations checking in Mainland China, you must acknowledge a strict hierarchy of statutory laws, administrative regulations, and technical codes overseen by the **Ministry of Housing and Urban-Rural Development (MOHURD)** and local Natural Resources/Construction bureaus.

The primary statutory framework and documentation required for planning submission and drawing review include:

---

### 1. Overarching National Laws (法律)

These are the foundational legislative acts passed by the National People's Congress that dictate legal liabilities, responsibilities, and procedural requirements:

* **Urban and Rural Planning Law of the PRC (中华人民共和国城乡规划法):** Governs spatial planning, land-use allocations, and the "Two Permits" system.
* **Construction Law of the PRC (中华人民共和国建筑法):** Regulates the entire construction process, including building permits, contractor/designer qualifications, and quality control.
* **Fire Protection Law of त्याचा PRC (中华人民共和国消防法):** Mandates statutory fire safety compliance for all building designs.

---

### 2. Core Administrative Regulations & Decrees (行政法规与部令)

These set the operational rules for handling planning permits and mandatory drawing reviews (施工图审查):

* **Regulations on Quality Management of Construction Engineering (建设工程质量管理条例):** Mandates that construction drawings must undergo independent third-party technical review (Construction Drawing Review / 施工图审查) before applying for a construction permit.
* **Administrative Measures for Construction Project Planning Permits (建设工程规划许可证管理办法):** Details the compliance parameters for project layouts, heights, and volumes.

---

### 3. Essential Statutory Submissions & Permits (法定报建证照)

In a Mainland workflow, your skill must account for the sequential acquisition of these mandatory project milestones:

* **Construction Land Planning Permit (建设用地规划许可证):** Confirms that the land-use aligns with the local controlled detailed planning (控制性详细规划).
* **Construction Project Planning Permit (建设工程规划许可证):** Granted after the architectural scheme design passes urban planning review. It legally seals the geometric, massing, and functional bounds of the project.
* **Construction Drawing Review Certificate (施工图设计文件审查合格书):** Issued after a certified third-party review agency checks the architectural, structural, MEP, and geotechnical drawings against mandatory national codes.

---

### 4. Mandatory National Technical Codes (国家强制性工程建设规范)

When checking drawings, standard practice requires compliance with a unified system of standards. Mentioning these specific code frameworks ensures technical validity:

* **General Specifications for Building Engineering and Construction (工程建设规范全文本 / 强制性工程建设规范):** The updated consolidated system of mandatory technical requirements (replacing older standalone forced clauses).
* **Unified Standard for Reliability Design of Engineering Structures (工程结构通用规范) & related structural codes.**
* **Code for Fire Protection Design of Buildings (建筑设计防火规范 - GB 50016):** The core statutory benchmark for layouts, compartmentation, and evacuation routes.
* **Accessbility, Energy Saving, and Green Building Codes (建筑节能与可再生能源利用通用规范, etc.):** Enforcing national carbon-peak and environmental targets.

---

**Key statutory documents to acknowledge for mainland China planning submission (规划报建 / 建设工程规划许可) and construction drawing / building regulations checking (施工图设计文件审查).**

Focus is on the national framework that applies across mainland China (local rules and detailed checklists add city/province specifics and must also be followed).

### 1. Core laws and regulations (highest-level statutory basis)

| Document | Key relevance |
|----------|---------------|
| **中华人民共和国城乡规划法** (Urban and Rural Planning Law) | Foundation for all planning permits (选址意见书, 建设用地规划许可证, 建设工程规划许可证). Requires design schemes and drawings to comply with detailed plans / planning conditions. |
| **中华人民共和国建筑法** (Construction Law) | Overall framework for construction quality, design responsibility, and permits. |
| **中华人民共和国消防法** (Fire Protection Law) | Fire safety design and review requirements. |
| **建设工程质量管理条例** (Regulations on the Quality Management of Construction Projects) | Quality and safety obligations of design units; basis for mandatory drawing review. |
| **建设工程勘察设计管理条例** (Regulations on the Administration of Survey and Design of Construction Projects) | Survey/design quality, seals/signatures, and review obligations. |
| **房屋建筑和市政基础设施工程施工图设计文件审查管理办法** (MOHURD Order No. 13, as amended 2015 & 2018) | **Core statutory instrument for construction drawing review.** Defines what must be reviewed, who can review, review content, and outcomes. |

These are the documents that a skill.md should explicitly acknowledge as the primary legal basis.

### 2. Construction drawing review – mandatory content (per Order No. 13)

Review institutions must check (at minimum):

- Compliance with **engineering construction mandatory standards** (工程建设强制性标准 / 强条).
- Safety of foundation and main structure.
- Fire safety.
- Civil air-defence (人防) protection safety (excluding command works).
- Compliance with civil building energy-saving mandatory standards; for green-building projects, also green-building standards.
- Whether survey/design enterprises and registered practitioners have properly sealed and signed the drawings.
- Other content required by laws, regulations or rules.

Review is mandatory for almost all new, expansion and reconstruction house-building and municipal infrastructure projects (with limited local exemptions for very small / low-risk works in some cities).

### 3. Planning submission side – key statutory / quasi-statutory documents

- Planning conditions / 规划设计要点 issued by the planning & natural resources authority.
- 建设用地规划许可证 and related land-use approval documents.
- Approved detailed plan / control detailed plan requirements.
- Local technical regulations on urban planning administration (e.g., Shanghai, Beijing, Shenzhen equivalents).
- Design scheme / architectural scheme approval documents (scheme review stage often precedes full construction drawings).

Drawings submitted for the **建设工程规划许可证** must match the approved planning conditions, red-line, setbacks, height, FAR, greening, parking, sunlight, etc.

### 4. Supporting standards and technical documents that must be referenced

- **Current national / industry mandatory standards and codes** (the “强条” system). Key families include:
  - General architectural: *民用建筑设计统一标准* GB 50352, *住宅设计规范* GB 50096, etc.
  - Fire: relevant GB 50016 and related codes.
  - Structure, seismic, energy-saving, accessibility (无障碍), green building, civil defence, etc.
- **建筑工程设计文件编制深度规定** (Depth of Design Documents for Construction Projects – 2016 edition or current).
- Local construction-drawing review technical guidelines / checklists (各省市施工图审查要点 or 导则). These operationalise the national Order and list the exact clauses checked.
- Drawing standards: *房屋建筑制图统一标准* GB/T 50001 and related professional drawing standards.

### 5. Practical notes for a skill.md

- Always state that **national laws + MOHURD Order No. 13** form the baseline; local planning & housing authorities issue additional technical provisions and checklists that take precedence for local submissions.
- Distinguish the two main stages:
  1. **Planning permit stage** (规划许可 / 报建) – compliance with planning conditions, overall layout, indicators.
  2. **Construction drawing review stage** (施工图审查) – technical compliance with mandatory standards, structure/fire/人防/energy safety.
- Emphasise that review focuses on public interest, public safety and mandatory standards; it is not a full design audit.
- Note the ongoing national reform toward digital / multi-department joint review and the trend of simplified or post-event checks for small low-risk projects (still subject to designer responsibility).
- Require that any skill output acknowledge the need to use the **version of standards valid at the time of the design contract** (with the one-year transition rule for new mandatory norms that many provinces apply).
