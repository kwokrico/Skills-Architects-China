# Statutory source catalog

Official pages and attachments cited by [cn-statutory-source-register.md](../../mainland-architect-master/references/cn-statutory-source-register.md). Retrieved 2026-11-19. A saved file is the government download, not a rewrite. Digests under `cn_s_reference/` still do not copy clauses from these files. How these instruments relate is drawn in [RELATIONSHIPS.md](RELATIONSHIPS.md).

GB is Guobiao (国家标准, national standard). MOHURD is the Ministry of Housing and Urban-Rural Development (住房和城乡建设部). NPC is the National People's Congress (全国人民代表大会).
